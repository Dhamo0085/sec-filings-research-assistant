What does Apple disclose about how it manages cybersecurity risk?

Answer

Apple's management, led by its Head of Corporate Information Security, has overall responsibility for identifying, assessing, and managing material risks from cybersecurity threats [1]. The Head of Corporate Information Security leads a dedicated Information Security team that develops policies and procedures, conducts employee training, implements security controls, assesses risk and compliance, monitors and responds to security events, and executes security testing [1]. The Company coordinates across teams to prevent and manage security incidents, engages third parties as appropriate, and uses a dedicated Supplier Trust team to manage risks from supplier relationships [1]. Apple has processes to log, track, address, escalate, and report cybersecurity incidents across the Company and its suppliers to senior management and the Audit and Finance Committee [1]. Additionally, Apple's enterprise risk management program reflects management's assessment of cybersecurity risks, and the Audit Committee assists the Board in overseeing and monitoring cybersecurity matters through regular reviews and discussions with management [1].
